# KGN RO Sale and Service: Design Preview

An independent responsive website proposal for KGN RO Sale and Service in
Rawatpur, Kanpur. This is not the business's official website.

## Preview and Hosting

- GitHub Pages preview: https://gopalshuklacanva.github.io/kgn-ro-sales-demo/
- Serve the repository root; the entry point is `index.html`.
- No build, packages, database, credentials or environment variables are needed.
- GitHub Pages publishes the `main` branch root. Keep `.nojekyll`.
- Keep the independent-preview footer, `noindex, nofollow` and `robots.txt`
  until an official launch is approved. The public demo and repository remain
  accessible to anyone with their URLs.

## Content

The business name, address, phone, hours, 5.0 rating and 30 reviews were checked
directly on Google Maps on 27 September 2026. They are a dated snapshot. Google
showed `Add website`, so no business website was listed at the time of checking.

The gallery contains six owner-posted images. The hero is an AI-edited
transparent cutout derived from the AquaX Swift owner image; the original is
also in the gallery. Product trademarks, photos and review excerpts retain
their original ownership. Obtain approval for ongoing official use.

See `SOURCES.md` for provenance. Manrope's license is included in
`assets/manrope-license.txt`.
